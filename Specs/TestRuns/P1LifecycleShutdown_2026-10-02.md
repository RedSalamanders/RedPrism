Date: 2026-10-02. Branch: main. Scope: qualified functional P1 integration; broader P1 and G1 remain open.

## Changes and independent expectations

Production New, New Canvas and Close prepare complete optional document/history publication on workers under
the selected host ordinal. Capture follows whole-graph admission and retains sealed authority/history roots;
it does not copy the old document records. Literal canvas, saved revision, Undo/Redo, private clipboard and
palette behavior, exact cancellation/fault preservation and younger-input ordering remain unchanged. A late
opacity-field acknowledgement cannot edit the replacement document.

Ordinary Ctrl+T, typed transform headers, body drags and layer nudges use deferred transform work. The existing
input-time geometry, persistent/ephemeral cancellation, distortion and floating-selection expectations remain.
Device replacement and shutdown before/after worker readiness preserve authority and retire owner tickets.

Scheduler close joins producers, drops owner-affine wrappers while its own release sink remains installed,
detaches borrowed application graphics, then runs explicit session CPU retirement on a preallocated final
MTA callback. Cancellation cannot suppress final retirement. Reservations, wake events and apartment ownership
survive through its acknowledgement; callbacks do not apply during shutdown. The standalone Application cases
own their actual hidden HWND/WARP/chrome and sink, rather than borrowing an outer test host's scheduler.

Folder staging publication allows at most three native rename attempts for errors 5/32, with lazy 25/100 ms
one-shot worker waits. Cancellation before rotation preserves the destination; after rotation publication
finishes or restores the complete backup. Native errors survive timer faults. Unexpected destination content
is never overwritten or cleaned. These limits bound attempts and scheduled waits, not an in-flight OS call.
