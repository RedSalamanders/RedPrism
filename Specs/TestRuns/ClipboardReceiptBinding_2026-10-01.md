## Scope

A fixed registered receipt binds process/session and private-copy serial to local immutable sizes/SHA-256 digests.
Prepare owns receipt/PNG/DIB movable blocks before Empty. Receipt places first; failure places no images. After a
receipt succeeds, complete both image attempts despite late stop. Report exact held sequence and native error.
Hash full initialized exposed blocks (including zero padding) and only owned incoming copies after CloseClipboard.
Current private key, receipt and every present copied image format must match; a receipt alone supplies no origin.
