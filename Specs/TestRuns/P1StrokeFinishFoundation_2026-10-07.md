## Integrated scope

`BrushStroke` can transfer its exclusive CPU draft while its graphics engine stays borrowed by the owner.
The worker prepares the final curve and tile list, installs exact uint coverage after readback, composes the
private tiles, and performs cancellable healing. Failed or incomplete detached drafts cannot expose snapshots.
The legacy curve, coverage shader, candidate order, healing math, and existing golden values remain references.

`GpuWorkQueue` now carries typed structured uint buffers through the existing bounded slots, staging budget,
fence/event waits, device generations, and exact mapped leases. Conversion into coverage bytes occurs on a
worker. `GpuBrushCoverage::RecordCoverage` records final coverage without the compatibility path's blocking
readback; application use is still pending.

`StrokeFinishInput` captures immutable source/history handles and the exact accepted opening. Its worker
factory builds the snapshot, carried mask, eager semantic mask thumbnail, sealed document, and history
publication. Owner publication compares scalar authority, immutable root/selection identities, and history
state before swapping complete owners. It neither visits pixels nor builds history. The old source/display
and private draft stay in the charged aggregate for worker retirement.

The finish marker prevents a younger brush. A direct reference Undo/Redo first cancels that marker, preserving
the older history entry. A settled-only history restore cannot capture or apply while the marker exists.
Application commands must instead remain queued behind their accepted original stroke through apply/drop.

Normal startup now arms the existing notification-driven history residency subscriber. Its actual `Run`
controls cover successful notification/trim, acquired-prefix failure, full subscription/route capacity,
rollback, repeated shutdown, and quit-message isolation. Whole H3 performance remains a separate P1 gate.
