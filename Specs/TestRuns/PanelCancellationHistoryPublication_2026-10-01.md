## Publication and ordering

request waits behind that work's terminal apply/drop acknowledgement. Repeated Cancel for the same document/opening
returns the retained pending observer before queue cancellation, so it cannot cancel itself or create another End.
Mutation controls remain frozen; the panel stays present until acknowledged publication.

Adjustment Cancel uses the admitted metadata closure with commit=false. It restores original parameters and prepares
End on the worker, then swaps complete document/history/panel/display/original ownership. Successful End advances
generation even when original records are unchanged. Destructive dismissal prepares a fixed authority/history view
and transfers panel/display ownership without restoring pixels, constructing history or advancing generation.
Both variants check exact lifetime/generation/sequence/opening, active/mask/crop and history epoch before mutation.
Construction failure retains panel/source/redo and the original HRESULT; stale input cannot close a newer opening.

caller gate. Prepared project/image replacement keeps its qualified discard-on-success/keep-draft-on-failure aggregate.
