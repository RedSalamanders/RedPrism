## Independent expectation and ownership

The owning selection contract requires an antialiased rectangle from (0,0) through (4,1) for a literal 4-by-1
canvas, coverage bytes {255,255,255,255}, and absent optional selection for Deselect. Explicit empty is a change;
repeated geometric equality is a no-op preserving revision, generation, history epoch and redo. Independent source
BGRA/Gray8 records, active target, mask state, crop and filter defaults remain unchanged. Changed publication adds
one named entry and one generation; Undo/Redo restore the exact optional selection. A named outer edit keeps its
depth and name; a later saved marker is honored without overwriting it from captured history state.

Sealed input owns the before-document, immutable history view and opaque settled-session authority. Moves remove
the source's authority. Private preparation owns the after-document and complete history publication; owner apply
checks the whole document token, target/mask/crop/session lifetime and history epoch, then uses nonthrowing swaps.
Replaced document/display/history owners remain in the prepared aggregate. Production scheduling and deferred release
are separate S2 work; destroying a core candidate on the owner is not an approved production retirement route.

Admission uses the existing conservative eight-record-copy history bound, fixed four-point result/control storage
and separately reserved retirement headroom. It does not call Clipper or create raster output. Record copying and
whole-history admission traversal remain H2/H3 costs. This bound cannot be reused for Boolean/offset commands.
