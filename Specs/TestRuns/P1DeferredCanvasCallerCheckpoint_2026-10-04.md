## Scope

Ordinary Brush/Eraser presses behind typed opacity closure capture document lifetime, target, mask palette,
settings, tool, modifiers and document-space coordinates at event time. The fixed stream retains its terminal
sample separately from its 512 queued samples. The owner replays one sample per action; an empty live stream
waits for another event. Escape and the first Undo/Redo cancel only the exact admitted stream/core opening.
Competing presses, capture/focus loss, failed closure and overflow have explicit outcomes. Accepted tool,
layer/target and standalone Save requests seal the accepted prefix only after successful admission.

The combined new controls include 52 first-pointer checks, 66 cancellation checks, 25 captured palette checks,
20 competing-input checks, 68 white-mask paint/erase checks and 96 boundary checks. Boundary cases reopen the
saved project and compare literal pixels and opacity. Mask erasure preserves the legacy selected white/black
paint rule. Exact history, receipt, captured settings, new-owner refusal and reservation checks remain intact.
No legacy golden is rebaselined. A mask/palette fixture restores its settings after settlement so the unchanged
later project-open suite tests its original environment.
