## DECISION: Remove Background — REJECTED for v1 (2026-09-21)

The legacy `SubjectRemoval.swift` is a thin call into Apple Vision's `VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest`;
the segmentation model ships with macOS. Windows has no OS-provided subject segmentation API. The faithful ports
are (a) bundling a segmentation model (u2net / RMBG-class, 40–170 MB) with onnxruntime and its DirectML execution
provider — a redistributed runtime, a model licence to clear, GPU-vendor failure modes and a pre/post-processing
pipeline that still could not claim parity with Vision's masks — or (b) a cloud service, which contradicts a local,
offline editor. Neither fits the v1 footprint (a portable ZIP, C15), and the STOP line forbids bundling
onnxruntime without a product decision. Decision: **no Remove Background command in v1** — no menu item, no no-op,
nothing that pretends. Revisit through a child plan (`RemoveBackground_<date>.md`) once a model with a compatible
licence is chosen; the hook is the filter session (a mask-producing kind beside `ContentAwareFill`, with
`FilterEdit::previewFailed` for "no subject found"). The user has the final say on this decision.
