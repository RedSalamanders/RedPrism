## Closeout (2026-09-21)

Shipped on the C10 stroke pipeline: strokes take a kind (Paint, Erase, Heal, Clone, Blur) and can compose from a
document-size sample (Clone Stamp's layer or composite, Blur's Gaussian-softened layer or mask, a warp's result
replacing the pixels under a hard tip); Spot Healing shows the legacy dark wash and runs the ported kernel at
mouse-up; Liquify and Smudge are `WarpStroke`, a document-size working copy dabbed in place and shown through the
stroke preview on the document grid; the Gradient is a pending `BrushStroke` canvas fill (`PaintCanvas`) previewed
through the composition until Apply, Enter or a switch; the Shape tool rasterizes its box through the exact-area
coverage rasterizer into a live shape layer (`ImageLayer::shapeImage`, `LiveShape`) that redraws when a transform
commits; the Eyedropper reads the flattened readback. Tip families park per tool as in the legacy session. The
session is one unit: HostTests and IoTests now compile the tool sources and link Clipper2 like the other projects.

Deviations from the legacy app, recorded in the owning specs: the Gradient header shows the two end colors as
swatches instead of a checkered gradient strip; Clone Stamp draws its source crosshair but not the legacy in-ring
preview of what a click would stamp; the rounded-rectangle live transform preview (`shapeTransformPreview`) is not
ported (the layer stretches until the commit redraws it); a healing or warp stroke's committed raster uploads
through the sparse path instead of adopting the working texture (the preview never showed the final pixels);
the eyedropper's sample ring is drawn by `CanvasOverlay` rather than a view of its own; the smear goldens are the
port's own (the legacy suite had none). The deferred C9 pixel edits (Cmd-drag pixel moves and nudges, Delete
clears pixels, fills, the floating selection transform, copy / cut / paste) remain open and now have their raster
primitives (`Fill`, `ClearPixels`, `AddPixelLayer`); they need an indexed plan before implementation. Gates:
`test.ps1` Debug, Release and ASan Debug, `build.ps1 -Platform ARM64 -Configuration Debug`, `format.ps1 -Check`,
`validate-skills.ps1`.
